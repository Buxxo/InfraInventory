UPDATE ONLY - does not contain your data
========================================
This package replaces the program files. It has NO Inventory, Issues or Stuff
folders, so your inventory.xlsx and everything you entered stay untouched.

How to install
  1. Close the page in the browser. Back up your InfraInventory folder (copy it).
  2. Copy these into your InfraInventory folder, replacing the existing files:
       index.html
       dashboard-template.html
       vendor\xlsx.full.min.js and vendor\LICENSE-xlsx.txt
  3. Open index.html, click "Connect project folder" (pick your InfraInventory
     folder). It loads Inventory\inventory.xlsx.
  4. Click "Save to Excel" once. This adds the new sheets and columns to your
     workbook (empty, with default lists) and refreshes index.html with your data.
     Existing rows are not changed.

New sheets added with defaults on first save: ActivityTypes, ActivityStatuses,
ServerStatuses (and BackupTypes, Environments, DataCenters, RackList if missing).
New ServersMI columns added empty: FQDN, vCenter name, Last patch CRQ.
